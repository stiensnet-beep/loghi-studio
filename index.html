Loghi-studio

Old Frisian handwriting, readable and searchable

Loghi-studio lets the computer read court books, orphan books and other archive records from Friesland, from the sixteenth to the nineteenth century. Browse the scans with the text beside them, search for any word, and have the names from your family tree traced by themselves.

The computer does not read without mistakes. A find only counts after it has been read on the scan itself.

      Keyboard shortcuts and explanation
      • ← and →: previous and next scan. ↑ and ↓: previous and next line; the scan zooms to that line.
      • + and −: zoom. 0: whole scan. W: full width. L: line boxes. C: uncertainty. Esc: let go of the line.
      • S: select a piece. Drag a box over the scan; the text of the lines in the box is shown beside the scan. Dragging with Shift held does the same. With the keyboard: Shift + ↑ or ↓ makes the piece larger or smaller from the chosen line. Esc: all lines again.
      • The text beside the scan can be made wider or narrower: drag the bar between the scan and the text, or use the buttons Text wider and Text narrower. The width is remembered.
      • The shortcuts work when the cursor is not in an input field. With a screen reader: put the focus on the scan first.
      • [?] before a line means: Loghi is unsure about this line. [??] means: very unsure. [name] means: a hit of the name finder.
      • [corrected] or [checked] before a line means: a person read this line on the scan. [partly corrected] means: a person corrected some words, the rest is as Loghi read it. [unreadable] means: a person could not read it.
      • E: correct the chosen line, or the first line of the scan that was not checked yet.
      • Text read by Loghi is a pointer. A find only counts after it has been read on the scan itself.

      Correct lines

      Read the line on the scan and make the text the same as what is written there. Every line you check is kept apart from the text of Loghi, and becomes a training line for a reading model that knows Frisian handwriting better.

      Names in the index for this scan

      Read by people, from the index of AlleFriezen. A help for reading the names. The index may spell a name differently: type what is written on the scan.

        Rules for typing, and the keyboard
        • Type what is written, letter by letter: keep the old spelling, the capitals and the punctuation. Do not make it modern and do not write out abbreviations.
        • A line that Loghi read right is saved as it is: that is a training line too.
        • Can you not read the line with certainty? Choose Unreadable and do not guess. A guessed line teaches the reading model mistakes.
        • Did you correct only one or a few words, and can you not read the rest of the line with certainty? Choose Save, only some words corrected. Your correction is shown in View and Search, but the line does not become a training line.
        • Enter: save and go to the next line. Alt + W: only some words corrected. Alt + U: unreadable. Alt + N: skip without saving. Alt + P: the line before.
        • The text of Loghi is never thrown away: withdraw a check and the line is as Loghi read it.

        Training set

        Makes training lines for Loghi from all checked lines of this collection: for every line its image, cut from the scan by Loghi itself, and its text.

        Search the text that was read

        Loose: all words must be in the line; ij is y, ck is k, ae is aa, doubled letters count as one. Fuzzy: in addition 1 or 2 letters may differ per word, because Loghi often reads names slightly wrong. After Loghi-studio has started, the search index is built in the background; a search in those first minutes can take longer.

        Names in old spellings: for a person. Each word is also searched in the old spellings the name finder knows (Pieter also Pyter and Pytter, Hayes also Hayesen and Hayesdr, c or k, z or s, v or f), and one or two letters may differ. When the collection has an index of names made by people, the deeds that name the person are listed first: those names were read by people, so they are found even where Loghi read the name wrong.

        Name finder

        Loghi-studio searches by itself, in the background, for the names on your lists of names. The hits in the chosen collection are below, per person; open a person to see the hits. You manage the lists of names further down this page. To search for any word, use the Search tab.

        Hits

        A hit is strong when the name is spelled exactly like that, or when Loghi is sure of the line. A hit is weak when the name only looks like it and the line is unsure. A hit is with a relative when a partner, parent or child of that person is named on the same scan. A name with more than 100 strong hits is a very common name. Every hit is a pointer: it only counts after it has been read on the scan.

        A hit is a possible reading error when one word of the name is there as usual and the other word only fits if Loghi made one of its typical reading errors, such as u for n, y for i or S for P. These hits are kept apart: you only see them when you choose them under Which hits.

          Import many names at once

          For a list of people from a family tree. Loghi-studio makes the search forms itself: first name with patronymic, first name with surname, and old spellings such as Pieter, Pyter and Pytter, or Hayes, Hayesz, Hayesen and Hayesdr. A person with only a first name is skipped. People with the same name become one name.

          After a name you may put a semicolon or a tab and a reference, for example the title of the page of that person in your family tree. A JSON file may also hold relatives; the tool tools_gedcom_names.py makes such a file from a GEDCOM file.
          The text {uid} is replaced by the reference of the person, so every person gets a link to the family tree.

          Jobs

          Reading queue

          The reading queue is switched off on this server: Loghi does not run here. Books are read on the own computer.

          Loghi reads the books in this queue one at a time, from top to bottom. A book is read in small chunks of scans, so after a stop or a restart only the chunk that was running is read again. A scan that fails is tried once more and skipped after the second failure.

          Manage collections

          Collections are managed on the own computer, because they point to folders on the disk. This server shows the collections that were copied to it.

          A collection is a folder with a sub-folder of scans for every book. Removing a collection from this list does not delete any scans or texts from the disk.

            Accounts

            Who may log in on this server. An admin sees everything and manages the lists of names and the accounts; a corrector may check lines; a viewer may look and search. A new password is shown once and is kept nowhere: pass it on yourself.

            The accounts

              New account

              If the only admin forgets the password: on the server itself, run python auth.py with the settings folder, the name and the role admin. That gives a new password.

              Loghi-studio

              To make a bookmark: press Ctrl + D. Loghi-studio must be running before the bookmark works; start it with start_loghi_studio.bat, or with a shortcut to that file.
